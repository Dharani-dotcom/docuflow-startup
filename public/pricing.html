Yes. Sandbox is free and includes a local workspace with an interactive documentation preview.

The Pro plan adds automated GitHub sync, support for unlimited remote repositories, and custom-domain hosting.

Can I change plans later?+

Choose the plan that fits today. The Sandbox and Pro Growth options are designed for different stages of your workflow.
